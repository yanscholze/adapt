## ADAPT-cover.png

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: original Roblox adventure game cover, landscape 16:9, 1920x1080 preferred.
Primary request: polished stylized promotional illustration for the original game ADAPT!, an adventure about transforming a small creature to awaken an archipelago.
Subject: one original simple friendly pale mint creature with a rounded egg-shaped torso, round head, two dark oval eyes, small teal paws and feet, angular lavender crystal wings, amber claws, subtle cyan fish tail. No clothes, no hair, no mouth required, no recognizable existing character. Its actual in-game design uses rounded primitives and four flat geometric wing segments; stay close to this simple construction.
Scene: turquoise archipelago ocean, green island in foreground, large ancient tree left far away, basalt volcano with warm orange caldera far right, three small floating islands climbing diagonally toward a pale violet crystal in the distance. A few mint glowing memory crystals on the grass. Warm afternoon sunlight, readable silhouettes, handmade low-poly game diorama aesthetic.
Composition: creature large in right lower third looking toward viewer, bold empty sky left upper third for text. Do not depict game interface or pretend this is a screenshot.
Text (verbatim): "ADAPT!" large cream bold playful clean lettering upper-left, and "EXPLORE. TRANSFORME. DESPERTE." below in smaller spaced readable capitals.
Color palette: dark navy, mint green, ocean teal, soft lavender, amber.
Constraints: cover art, original intellectual property only; no Roblox logo, no copyrighted characters, no photo realism, no visual clutter, no watermark.
